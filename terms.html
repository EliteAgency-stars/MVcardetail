3. Payment

  • Payment is due when the service is completed, after you have had the chance to inspect the vehicle.
  • We accept debit and credit cards.

4. Rescheduling, cancellations and weather

  • If you need to reschedule or cancel, please message us on WhatsApp as early as possible so the slot can be offered to another customer.
