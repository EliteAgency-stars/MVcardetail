Questions, answered.

Something else? Ask us on WhatsApp.

Do you come to me?

Yes. We work at your home, office, marina or hangar in Miami-Dade, Broward and Charlotte. You can also bring your car to our shop at 3937 Pembroke Rd, Hollywood, FL.

What do I need to have ready?

Access to the vehicle and permission to work at the location. Please remove valuables. Someone should hand us the car at the start and check it with us at the end.

How long does a detail take?

From about 45 minutes for an Exterior Wash to around 4 hours for a Premium Detail. Larger or very dirty vehicles can take longer.

Why do prices say "from"?

Each service has a price for sedans and coupes, SUVs and crossovers, and trucks, large SUVs and vans. Pet hair, heavy stains or excessive dirt can change it, and we always confirm the price with you before we start.

How do I pay?

After the service, once you have inspected the car. We accept debit and credit cards.

Can you remove pet hair and stains?

Interior and Premium Details include light stain treatment. Heavy stains, deep extraction and excessive pet hair are quoted separately so you know the price upfront.

Do you offer ceramic coating and paint correction?

Yes. We also do buffing, polishing and headlight restoration. These are quoted per vehicle: send us photos on WhatsApp.

Do you detail boats and aircraft?

Yes. Besides cars, SUVs and trucks, we work at marinas and hangars. Message us for a quote.

What products do you use?

Professional-grade products. We are Meguiar's certified and have been detailing since 2018.

What if it rains or I need to reschedule?

Message us on WhatsApp as early as possible. If the weather makes exterior work unsafe, we will contact you to move the appointment.

What if I'm not happy with the result?

Tell us before we leave or within 24 hours with photos. We will review it with you and make it right where reasonable.
